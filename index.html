01 — About

Who We Are

GongYu Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian-based company specialising in the manufacturing of smart solar panel products and related equipment, including inverters and monitoring systems. We also provide installation, maintenance and technical support services.

🌱

Our Vision

To become a trusted Malaysian manufacturer of smart solar solutions for a cleaner and more energy-efficient future.

☀️

Our Mission

To achieve customer satisfaction by producing reliable smart solar products, providing professional installation and after-sales services, developing skilled employees and continuously improving our technology.
